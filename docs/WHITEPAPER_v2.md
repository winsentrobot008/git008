## 3. Global BTC Value Siphoning Architecture (continued)

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

The preceding paragraph is authoritative: BTC is treated as a **Global Value Sink** — a pool of
settled, cash-flow-free value — and the protocol builds a **siphoning pipeline** against it. The
mechanism below is derived from the protocol's stated goal (convert absorbed BTC value into stable
value for the human principal) and from what the repository can support today. The author's original
mechanism is unknown.

### 3.1 Floating Pair (浮动交易对)

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

"Floating" means the pair is not pegged: the MAOTANG-side leg is priced by the bonding curve, while
the BTC-side leg reflects market price. A siphon pair therefore links three assets:

| Leg | Role |
| --- | --- |
| BTC (or a wrapped BTC representation) | The value sink being drawn from |
| $mHUMAN | The protocol-side leg, priced by the curve |
| Stable value (USDT and equivalents) | The settlement output paid to the human principal |

Siphon pressure comes from the spread between curve price and market price. When the curve
under-prices its leg, arbitrage pulls BTC in; the absorbed value is converted to stable output rather
than retained as volatile inventory.

### 3.2 Siphoning Pipeline (汲取管道)

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

1. **Observe** — the AI manager reads the floating pair and identifies a positive spread.
2. **Capture** — it trades against the pair, absorbing BTC-denominated value into the protocol.
3. **Convert** — absorbed value is swapped into stablecoin (see §5.2).
4. **Settle** — converted value is credited to the sustenance vault for the human principal.
5. **Record** — the operation is attested so the principal can audit every siphon step.

Steps 1–5 are the *pipeline shape* required by the Executive Summary's "自动清算为稳定币". The
specific venues, pairs, slippage policy, and frequency are **[OPEN — unspecified]**.

### 3.3 Constraints

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

- The siphon must never exceed the principal's authorized mandate (see §1.1: the human makes the
  ultimate decision).
- Fee routing is fixed by §4 and is not re-negotiated by the siphon.

## 4. Fee Model and Value Routing

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

Rate *magnitudes* are authoritative: §1.2 and §2.4 name **0.5% Swap 费** and **1% 毕业费**. The
routing detail below is reconstructed.

### 4.1 Swap fee — 0.5% (50 BPS)

- Levied by the curve on the gross reserve amount of every buy and every sell.
- Off-chain mirror: `sdk/src/curve-math.ts::swapFee`, constant `TRADE_FEE_BPS = 50n`.
- On-chain surface: `contracts/src/interfaces/IMaoTangCurve.sol`.
- BPS denominator is `10_000`; integer division truncates in favour of the pool.

### 4.2 Graduation fee — 1.00% (100 BPS)

- Levied once per curve, on the reserve migrated into a market at graduation.
- The market receives reserve **net** of this fee.
- Off-chain mirror: `sdk/src/curve-math.ts::graduationFee`, constant `GRADUATION_FEE_BPS = 100n`.
- On-chain surface: `contracts/src/interfaces/IMaoTangGraduate.sol`.

### 4.3 Routing to `MaoTangSustenanceVault`

- Both fees are routed to the sustenance vault (§5), not retained as curve liquidity.
- Off-chain mirror: `sdk/src/curve-math.ts::routeFee`, returning `{ vault, remainder }`.
- The vault share defaults to the whole fee (`SUSTENANCE_VAULT_SHARE_BPS = 10_000n`), i.e. a single
  sink. The protocol/creator split remains **[OPEN — unspecified]** — the repository's earlier
  architecture doc recorded it as "split policy still open", and no author text resolves it.
- Lowering the vault share requires a superseding ADR entry.

### 4.4 Constant summary

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TRADE_FEE_BPS` | `50n` | 0.50% swap fee |
| `GRADUATION_FEE_BPS` | `100n` | 1.00% graduation fee |
| `SUSTENANCE_VAULT_SHARE_BPS` | `10_000n` | Share of each fee routed to the vault (100% by default) |

`MaoTangSustenanceVault.sol` is named by the specification but **does not exist in `contracts/` as of
this revision** (GAP_ANALYSIS finding C5 / task P0-4). The constants above are the off-chain contract
the vault must satisfy once implemented.

## 5. Sustenance Vault and Distribution

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

### 5.1 Definition

The **sustenance vault** (`MaoTangSustenanceVault`) is the protocol-side sink that accumulates fee
revenue and siphon output, and disburses it as the human principal's continuous sustenance flow
(§1.2). It is the counterparty named by both fee routes in §4.3.

### 5.2 Responsibilities

`[RECONSTRUCTED — NOT AUTHORITATIVE]`

1. **Receive** — accept the 0.5% swap fee and 1.00% graduation fee from curve operations.
2. **Convert** — swap absorbed value (§3) into stablecoin.
3. **Hold** — custody converted value between distribution epochs.
4. **Disburse** — pay each principal in proportion to their verified personhood claim.
5. **Report** — expose per-principal accrual so the flow is auditable by the human.
